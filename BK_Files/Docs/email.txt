Sample Email that they normally sent, with details:

Dear Investor,

Thank you for choosing Cedar Capital Limited!

Please find below the key information regarding buying shares, as well as the steps required to open a brokerage account with us and the Reserve Bank of Malawi’s Central Depository System (CSD), where all shares are held electronically.

 

Benefits of Buying Shares:

Capital gains – Selling shares at a higher price than you purchased them.
Dividends – Earning a portion of company profits paid to shareholders.
Liquidity – Sell shares anytime at market value to access cash.
Voting rights – Participate in company decisions by attending AGMs.
Diverse investment – Purchase shares in more than one listed company.
Long-term value – A solid option for wealth preservation over time.
Disadvantages to Consider:

Price fluctuations – Share value can decline based on performance.
No guaranteed returns – Share prices and dividends can vary.
Market risk – Oversupply or poor company performance may impact returns.
Next Steps – Account Opening:

To proceed, you will need to complete the following forms (attached):

Cedar Capital Account Corporate Opening Form
Central Depository System (CSD) Form
Key Facts Statement (Please review carefully before signing)
Residential Address Verification Form (if applicable)
You are required to complete all pages of the Cedar account opening form and initial all pages.

 

 

Required Supporting Documents for Company

– Certificate of Incorporation; and

– Notice of Registered Office and Postal Address; and

– Identification document(s) of the person(s) managing the Company; and

– Resolution specifying who is authorised to act on behalf of the Company; and

– Identification document(s) of the person(s) authorised to act on behalf of the Company; and

– Proof of address of both authorised person/s managing the company and authorised persons per the Resolution to act on behalf of the company

-3 months recent bank statement, in the name of the Company.

🔹 If your utility bill is not in your name, you may instead:

Complete the attached Residential Address Verification Form, or
             Provide a signed tenancy or lease agreement.

📬 If you are far from our offices or would prefer to submit documents electronically, feel free to print, fill in, scan, and email the completed forms and documents back to us.

Once we receive all the necessary information, we’ll proceed with opening your accounts and will notify you as soon as they are active and ready for trading.

Please note that the Malawi Stock Exchange no longer issues paper share certificates. All shares are now held in electronic format via the CSD, and you will receive a shareholding statement as proof of ownership.

You are free to choose which shares to invest in from any of the 16 listed companies on the exchange. We’re here to guide you if you need help with this decision.

Should you have any questions or need assistance during the process, don’t hesitate to reach out.
